Principles applied

  • One primary action per screen (the filled button).
  • Every step can be undone: Back zooms out, Reset clears a tap, Skip is always offered.
  • The 3D view has a list alternative. Voice is an addition to typing, never the only way.
  • Icon-only nav has names for screen readers. Tap targets are 44pt; at this mock's scale that is 28px.
  • Optional fields say they are optional and why we ask.
  • Calm, plain wording. No medical advice.

What to check when testers use it

  • Can they log a symptom from the hologram without help?
  • Do they find the "+" and the Browse search on their own?
  • Do they attach a photo to a visit?
