Trace design system

Modern and futuristic, but quiet. Graphite surfaces, bracket frames, small mono labels and a single teal glow that marks whatever you can act on. Version 3. Colors come from the MUI palette.

Rules

One action
One filled button per screen. The rest are outlined or text.
Teal means act
Accent marks what is selected, active or tappable on the hologram. Nothing else.
Undo
Back, Reset and Skip are always reachable.
Alternatives
3D has a list. Voice has typing. Color has words.
Tone
Short, plain, kind. Never diagnose.
Tokens only
No one-off colors, sizes or radii in the app.
