Rules that keep it consistent

One action
One filled button per screen. Everything else is outlined or text.
Undo
Back, Reset and Skip are always reachable.
Alternatives
3D has a list. Voice has typing. Color has words.
Tone
Short, warm, plain. Never diagnose.
Tokens only
No one-off colors, sizes or radii in the app.
