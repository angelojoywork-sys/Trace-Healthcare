# 07 · Research instruments (ready for validation)

Not run yet. Use these to replace proto-personas with real data. Get informed consent; don't collect identifiable health details you don't need; store notes securely.

---

## A · Interview guide (30–40 min, 5–8 participants)

**Recruit:** 4–5 women 20–45 living in Ireland (at least 2 who moved here in the last 5 years), 1–2 men, 1–2 trans or non-binary people, 1 partner/carer. Mix of medical card / no card. Optional: 1 GP, 1 dentist, 1 pharmacist.

**Intro (3 min):** purpose, consent, can skip any question, no right answers.

**1 · Recent episode (10 min)**
- Tell me about the last time you had a symptom that didn't go away.
- What was the first thing you did? Who did you ask? What did you search?
- How did you decide where to go? What nearly stopped you?
- What did it cost? Did cost change what you did?

**2 · The visit (8 min)**
- Walk me through the appointment. How did you explain what was wrong?
- Did you feel listened to? Why / why not?
- What did they tell you? What do you remember now?
- How did you keep track afterwards (notes, photos, leaflets, nothing)?

**3 · Next time (5 min)**
- Have you had to explain the same history to another clinician? How did that go?
- If you could hand them one thing, what would be on it?

**4 · AI and trust (6 min)**
- Have you used a symptom checker or chatbot for health? What happened?
- What would make you trust or distrust an AI asking about your symptoms?
- How would you feel about speaking a voice note after an appointment?

**5 · Privacy and identity (4 min)**
- What would you never put in a health app? Who should never see it?
- Are there questions apps ask about sex or gender that bother you?

**Concept probe (4 min):** show the one-line concept + 3 sketches (later phase). "What's useful? What's missing? What worries you?"

## B · Survey (5 min, aim n ≥ 50)

1. Age range · County · Years living in Ireland
2. Gender (free text, optional) · Pronouns (optional)
3. Do you have: medical card / GP visit card / private insurance / none / not sure
4. In the last 12 months, did you have a symptom you weren't sure who to see about? (Y/N)
5. Where did you go first? (Pharmacy / GP / Dentist / Out-of-hours / ED / Online / Nobody)
6. Looking back, was that the right place? (Yes / No / Not sure)
7. Did cost delay or stop you seeking care? (Never / Once / Several times)
8. Did you know about: free PRSI dental check-up / pharmacist treatment for common conditions / free contraception 17–35 / GP out-of-hours co-ops? (multi-select)
9. After an appointment, how much do you usually remember? (1–5)
10. Have you ever had to repeat your history to a new clinician and found it hard? (1–5)
11. Have you felt your pain or symptoms weren't taken seriously? (Never / Sometimes / Often)
12. How comfortable would you be describing symptoms to an AI assistant? (1–5) Why?
13. Which would you use? (multi-select) Symptom chat · Body map · Where-to-go guide · Voice recap after visits · Show-my-doctor screen · Reminders · Daily feeling check
14. What would stop you using a health app? (free text)
15. Anything else? (free text) · Happy to be interviewed? (email, optional)

## C · Usability test plan (for the prototype phase)

**Tasks**
1. "Your tooth has hurt for 4 days and now your ear aches. Use the app to find out where to go." *(success: dentist, 1–2 days)*
2. "Your face has started to swell and you feel feverish." *(success: red-flag screen reached)*
3. "You've just left the dentist. Record what happened." *(scripted voice note; success: meds, dose, follow-up captured)*
4. "You're at a new dentist. Show them your history." *(success: show-doctor view in ≤ 3 taps)*
5. "Change your pronouns and delete your data." *(success: found without help)*

**Measures:** completion, time on task, errors, SEQ per task, SUS, trust (1–5), think-aloud notes.
