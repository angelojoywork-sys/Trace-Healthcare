# 08 · Naming

**Recommendation: keep "Trace".**

It describes the core job in one word: you **trace** a symptom to its cause, **trace** the right route through the system, and keep a **trace** of every visit. It's short, gender-neutral, calm, and works as a verb ("Trace it").

Risks: "trace" also means contact-tracing (COVID Tracker app associations in Ireland) and "trace amounts". Mitigate with a descriptor: **Trace · your health, remembered** or **Trace Health**.

## Alternatives considered

| Name | Why it could work | Watch-outs |
|------|-------------------|------------|
| **Tend** | Warm, care-focused, verb ("tend to yourself") | Soft; less about memory |
| **Thread** | The story that connects each visit; good for timeline metaphor | Used by Meta (Threads) |
| **Kept** | "Everything the doctor said, kept." Strong on the memory promise | Weak on the routing job |
| **Cara** | Irish for "friend"; local, warm, feminine-leaning but neutral in use | Common name; existing health brands likely; check trademark |
| **Ask Eve** | Women-first and conversational | Gendered; conflicts with inclusive aim |

**Trademark/domain checks** (EUIPO, Irish Patents Office, App Store, .ie) are needed before committing to any name. `[to do]`

Taglines to test:
- "Describe it. Find the right door. Remember what they said."
- "Your health, remembered."
- "From 'what is this?' to 'here's my story'."
