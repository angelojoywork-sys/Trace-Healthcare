Thank you! [your name]

## What to try (about 5 minutes)

1. **Log how you feel.** Pretend something hurts. Turn the body, tap the spot, add a short note (you can type or use the mic), then say how bad it is.
2. **Log another one**, this time using "List view" under the body.
3. **Add a GP visit.** On Summary, open a log. Add a GP name, a date, and what the doctor said. Attach any photo (it can be a photo of anything, it does not have to be a real prescription).
4. **Find it again.** Go to Browse and search for something you logged.
5. **Share it.** Open a visit and tap the share icon. Save the PDF or send it to yourself.
6. **Find a GP** (optional). Open the pin tab and try the search or "Use my location".

## What I'm measuring, and what I'm not

- This is a test version. I count how the app is used: which screens you open, how long things take, whether a step worked.
- I do **not** see your symptoms, notes, GP names, photos or search words. Those stay on your phone.
- The only thing I read in your own words is the optional comment at the end. Please don't put health details or names in it.
- To remove everything the app saved and start over: open https://trace-healthcare-vvkf.vercel.app/?reset (or clear the site data for this page in your browser settings).

## Good to know

- Please use a recent phone browser (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android).
- Location and microphone are optional. Say no if you prefer and use the other options.
- The GP list is a partial list (about a fifth of Irish practices, best for Dublin). It is not a complete directory.
- Trace is a prototype, not medical advice. In an emergency, call 112 or 999.
