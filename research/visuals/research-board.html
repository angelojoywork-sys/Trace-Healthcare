The spine of the product

Three moments nobody joins up

Before the visit

“What is this, and where do I go?”

AI intake with a tap-to-point body map, then an Irish routing card: pharmacy, GP, dentist, GP co-op, injury unit or ED, with cost and entitlements.

Right after the visit

“What did they say?”

Speak a 60-second recap. Trace structures it into symptoms, advice, medicines and dose, next steps, follow-up date and how you feel.

The next visit

“Here’s my story so far.”

A one-screen show-my-doctor view: the issue, how long, what’s been tried, current medicines, last advice.

Safety path · overrides every screen

Red flag → Call 112 / 999 or go to the ED now

Spreading facial swelling, trouble breathing or swallowing, high fever with swelling, chest pain or stroke signs stop the conversation. Out of hours, Trace points to the regional GP co-op, the Dublin Dental Hospital emergency line, or the ED.

Design principles

Eight rules for every screen

  1. Ask, don’t tell.
    The AI interviews. It never names a diagnosis.
  2. Point to a real door.
    Every chat ends with an Irish next step, cost and HSE source.
  3. Safety interrupts.
    Red flags take over the screen instantly.
  4. Capture now, tidy later.
    Voice in, structured summary out. The user confirms.
  5. One screen for the doctor.
    Readable across a desk in 10 seconds.
  6. Private, visibly.
    On-device, no trackers, lock, export and delete.
  7. Calm, plain, kind.
    Plain English, large targets, no blame.
  8. Every body.
    Neutral map and language. Women’s health surfaced, never imposed.
